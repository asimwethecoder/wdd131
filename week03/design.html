The Purpose of Design

User-Centric Design

Effective web design focuses heavily on the user. Understanding visitor goals, behaviors, and pain points allows developers to build intuitive, engaging, and frictionless digital experiences. Clarity and consistency anchor great web design.

Notice the Contrast

This callout box demonstrates high-contrast text and corrected padding to meet WCAG AA and AAA accessibility guidelines, replacing inaccessible low-contrast colors.

Core Principles

  • Typography: Limit font choices and stick to clean, legible text weights.
  • Whitespace: Keep pages uncluttered to guide visitor focus effectively.
  • Visual Hierarchy: Use size, weight, and color to show relative importance.
  • Accessibility: Ensure content can be consumed by all users, including those utilizing assistive technologies.
